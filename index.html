About ARNWE

— ✦ —

ARNWE - Music for moments that stay.

There's a WE in ARNWE - because music is never just about one person. It's about us.

About love and trust.

About family and memories.

About the moments that change us, the feelings that connect us, and sometimes the things happening in the world around us.

ARNWE turns these moments into music — stories to feel, remember, and make your own.

Three languages. One identity.

Black Cover Band – English

Beige Cover Band – German

Red Cover Band – Albanian

Different words, same emotions.

PRIVACY POLICY

ARNWE — Privacy Policy
Last updated: 20 October 2026

About this website

ARNWE is an independent music project based in Switzerland. This website shares music, release information and links to official social media and streaming profiles.

Website access and hosting

When you visit a website, technical data such as your IP address, browser information and access time may be processed by the hosting provider to deliver the site, maintain security and operate the service. This site is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages, a service operated by GitHub. For information about GitHub’s data practices, see GitHub’s privacy statement. Hosting details will be confirmed when the website goes live.

No contact forms or analytics

This website does not include a contact form, user account, newsletter subscription or ARNWE-operated visitor analytics. ARNWE does not intentionally set advertising or tracking cookies through this website.

External links

Links to Spotify, YouTube, TikTok and Instagram take you to third-party services. Their own privacy policies and data processing practices apply once you visit them. This website does not embed their players or social feeds.

Contact and privacy requests

For questions about this website or privacy-related requests, contact ARNWE through the official Instagram or TikTok profile. Messages sent on those platforms are also subject to the respective platform’s privacy practices.

Changes

This notice may be updated if the website’s features, hosting or data processing change.
